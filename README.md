# Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin

The Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin is a dark, gothic-themed character design featuring a black oversized hoodie with crimson-red details and a large raven emblem on the back. The character has long, thick midnight-black curly hair with burgundy-red, deep wine-colored, silver-white, and champagne-toned streaks. The face is pale with honey-brown eyes and a black face covering over the lower half. The sleeves are oversized and slightly textured, ending in pale white cuffs. The character wears loose-fitting dark indigo-blue trousers with charcoal-black shading and small decorative accents. The shoes are predominantly black with pale white soles and toe details. This skin combines raven symbolism, crimson accents, dark clothing, and contrasting hair streaks to create a distinctive gothic, mysterious, and slightly rebellious Minecraft character.

## Install

1. Download the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin from the GitHub repository.
2. Open the Minecraft Launcher.
3. Click on the "Installations" tab.
4. Click on the "New Installation" button.
5. Name the installation "Gothic Raven" and click "Create".
6. Click on the "..." button next to the new installation and select "More installation options".
7. In the "Game Options" tab, click on "More Game Options".
8. In the "Resource Packs" section, click on "Open resource pack folder".
9. Copy the downloaded Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin into the resource pack folder.
10. Back in the Minecraft Launcher, click on the "Resource Packs" button.
11. Enable the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin resource pack.
12. Click on the "Play" button to start Minecraft with the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin.

## Use

1. Launch Minecraft with the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin installed.
2. Click on the "Skin" button in the main menu.
3. Select the "Gothic Raven" skin from the list of available skins.
4. Click on the "Done" button to apply the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin.
5. Start a new game or load an existing world to use the Gothic Raven Minecraft Skin.
